Made for the seat gap

A small accessory for an easy-to-miss space

The seat-to-console gap can be awkward to reach when a small item slips down. This organizer is intended to make that space more useful and keep everyday essentials closer at hand.

01

Keep small items nearby

Use the organizer as a place for suitable small essentials you want within reach when parked or before setting off.

02
