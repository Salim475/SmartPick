Questions before you order?

How do I place an order?

Choose a product page and tap Order now. WhatsApp opens with the product name in a prepared message. Send the message and we will discuss availability, payment and delivery details with you.

What are the listed prices?

The current listed prices are ₦18,000 for the seat gap organizer, ₦10,000 for the Sinofly seat-back organizer and ₦15,000 for the trunk organizer. Confirm current availability with us before payment.

Does the organizer fit every car?

Vehicle seats, consoles and trunks vary. Send your car make, model and year plus a clear photo of the area you want to organize. We can discuss the fit before you order, but please confirm any measurements or fit details with us directly.

How do I choose between the three products?

The gap organizer is for the space beside a front seat; the seat-back organizer is for storage behind a front seat; the trunk organizer is for grouping items in the boot. Each product page has photos and more detail.

Do you deliver across Nigeria?

Yes. SmartPick offers delivery across Nigeria. Share your state and area on WhatsApp so we can confirm the expected timing for your location.

Is delivery free?

Free delivery is currently advertised on the site. Confirm the delivery arrangement for your location with us on WhatsApp before completing your order.

How long will delivery take?

Timing depends on the destination and current delivery arrangements. We will provide an estimate on WhatsApp before confirming the order.

Can I order more than one item?

Yes. List the products and quantities you want in your WhatsApp message. We will confirm availability before you proceed.

What if my parcel arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact us on WhatsApp promptly and include clear photos of the parcel and item. We will review the issue and explain the next steps. See Shipping & Returns for more information.

Can I pay on this website?

No. This is a product catalogue, not an online checkout. Payment instructions are discussed with you directly on WhatsApp.
